# Biology and rest rhythms

Biology constrains **sleep and recovery**; it does **not** uniquely define a seven-day civil week.

## Circadian (~24 h)

- Endogenous period in humans is often **slightly longer than 24 h** (~24.2 h) under constant conditions.
- Entrainment by **light** (and social cues) pulls it to the solar day.
- **Implication:** The primary biological anchor for daily life is **day/night**, not week length. Any week must **compose** whole circadian days for global scheduling unless we accept drifting boundaries.

## Ultradian (minutes to hours)

- Human sleep alternates **NREM/REM** with a mean ultradian period around **90–110 minutes** (individual and method dependent).
- **Implication:** Sub-day structure exists, but it does not suggest 5, 6, 7, or 8-day weeks. It suggests **within-day** rest architecture.

## Infradian (multi-day, less than a year)

Evidence includes:

- **Sleep duration** variability with multi-day periods (roughly 2–18 d reported in some cohorts; not one fixed universal period).
- **Yearlong EEG (2025 preprint):** subject-specific “ultralong” sleep cycles of roughly **8–60 days**, including patterns near **~8, ~21, ~31, and ~56 days** - highly individual, not a planetary clock.
- **Menstrual cycle:** often near **29.5 d**, close to synodic month; relevant to **month-scale** biology, not week divisors.

## Circaseptan (~7 d) - cautious reading

Some studies report **about-seven-day** rhythms in physiology (e.g. heart rate in prolonged isolation) with period **not exactly 7.0 d** and confidence intervals excluding a precise seven-day trial period in at least one classic isolation study.

Reviews note:

- Persistence under some constant conditions suggests **endogenous components** in some species and humans.
- **Mood** weekly rhythms often **track work/weekend structure**; when that structure is removed, **7-day mood cycles can disappear** (supporting cultural entrainment for some variables).
- No clear **evolutionary advantage** of a 7 d clock is established comparable to circadian or circannual systems.
- Weak **cosmic** 7 d forcing is not a standard physical mechanism.

**Epact stance:** Treat **7** as a **strong lunar-quarter approximation** and a **possible biological weak prior**, not as proof that nature “chose” seven. Integer **6** and **8** remain competitive on divisibility and approximate quarters ([scoring.md](../00-method/scoring.md)).

## Rest density is independent of week length

Biology needs **recovery**; culture encodes **1 day off per N work days**. Those are separate knobs:

| Pattern | Work : rest | Notes |
|---------|-------------|--------|
| 6 + 1 | 6 days work, 1 rest | Rest fraction 1/7 of cycle if cycle is 7. |
| 5 + 2 | 5 + 2 | Rest fraction 2/7; not tied to lunar month. |
| 1 in 5 | 4 + 1 | Solar5 systems may use this. |

A time system should specify **rest rule** explicitly in [02-units/rest.md](../02-units/rest.md), not inherit it from week length.

## What biology adds to the search

1. **Hard constraint:** align long cycles with **integer solar days** for circadian lock (unless dual-overlay).
2. **Soft preference:** periodic **full recovery** at some ratio; no unique integer from physiology alone.
3. **Negative result:** rejecting “biology proves 7” is as important as any astronomical table.
