# Open questions

Items that **logic and nature underdetermine** - no unique answer without human values.

## Closed (historical, not proof)

**Did every civilization independently choose 7?** **No.** Many used other lengths; global sameness is largely **lock-in** (copying the 7-day package), while lunar quarters can independently suggest ~7 or ~8. Description only - [why-seven.md](why-seven.md).

## 1. Which “month” is canonical?

Synodic (visible), tropical (ecliptic), or twelfth of tropical year? Epact defaults synodic for Moon-facing systems, but **coastal tides** in diurnal regimes weight **declination** (~13.66 d half-cycle) differently.

## 2. Faithful phase vs midnight snap

Lune mode A vs B ([lune.md](../04-systems/lune.md)): astronomy vs appointments. How much phase error (hours) is acceptable globally?

## 3. Is W=6 a missing sixth system?

**Resolved:** [hex.md](../04-systems/hex.md) documents W=6 (Score_int leader; **Y** stronger than 7; weak **S**). Still not a unique optimum - see [weight-sensitivity.md](../03-search/weight-sensitivity.md).

## 4. Rest fraction as law

Biology requires recovery; **how much** (1/7 vs 2/7) is social choice. Should Epact normatively prescribe rest density?

## 5. Global vs local time

One week grid for UTC, or **latitude bands** with local photoperiod indices? Polar regions break “day = daylight.”

## 6. Naming and cognition

Ordinals vs phase names vs binary - which minimizes error in human use? Not derivable from astronomy.

## 7. Leap week vs epact day

Intercalation aesthetics: rare leap **week** vs frequent **orphan days**. Legal and software consequences differ; physics does not choose.

## 8. Long-term secular drift

Mean synodic month and tropical year **change** over millennia (tidal acceleration of Earth spin, etc.). How often should constants be retuned?

## 9. Menstrual–lunar coupling

~29.5 d cycle near synodic month - include in scoring weights for health apps, or keep civil/lunar separate (Dual)?

## 10. Three-cycle continued fractions

**Answered (integer 7/8 weeks):** 19 y = 235 mo = 940 wk = 6940 d with 580×7 + 360×8 and 110×29 + 125×30 month lengths - see [metonic-week-grid.md](../03-search/metonic-week-grid.md). Still **no fixed single W**; period-2 week values required.
